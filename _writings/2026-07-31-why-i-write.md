---
title: Why an engineer keeps a notebook
category: blog
date: 2026-07-31
excerpt: Code and writing are the same craft wearing different clothes.
---

I spend my days making machines agree with each other. Distributed systems,
retries, the slow art of getting two services to trust a third. It is precise
work, and I love it.

But precision has a cost: it trains you to delete yourself from the sentence.
Logs don't have a narrator. A pull request has no *I*. After enough years of
that, you can forget you have a voice at all.

So I write. Quotes when a thought arrives fully formed. Poems when it refuses
to. Blogs when I've understood something well enough to be wrong about it in
public. The same muscle that names a variable well names a feeling well —
both are about finding the shortest true thing.

This site is the two halves in one place. Flip to **Developer** for the work
that pays rent. Flip to **Clean** for the work that keeps me honest.
