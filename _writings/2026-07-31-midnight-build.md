---
title: Midnight build
category: poem
date: 2026-07-31
excerpt: For everyone who has watched a progress bar at 2 a.m.
---

The office hums a single note,
the fans, the fridge, the tired code.
A cursor blinks like a distant boat
that somehow knows the way back home.

I feed the machine another prayer —
a semicolon, a second chance.
It answers green. I lean back, aware
that hope compiles. And so I dance.
