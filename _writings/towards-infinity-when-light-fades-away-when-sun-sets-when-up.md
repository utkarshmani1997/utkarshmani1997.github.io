category: poem
lang: en
title: "Towards Infinity"
---
When the light fades away,
When the sun sets,
When the city lights up,
When it's time to get some rest,
Then the melancholy knocks the door,
And I start my journey towards infinity.

I close my eyes and ruminate,
I walk in every direction,
I run after the mirage,
I fly without the wings,
I swim across the oceans,
And I continue my journey towards infinity,
just to find a glimpse of you.
